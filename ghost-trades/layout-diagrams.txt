CARD HOVER STATES:
┌──────────────────────┐
│ CARD (Rest State)    │  ← 1px border, subtle shadow
└──────────────────────┘

      ↓ (Hover)

┌──────────────────────┐
│ CARD (Hover)         │  ← translateY(-4px), enhanced shadow
│ ▌ (Gradient bar)     │  ← Left accent glows
└──────────────────────┘


BUTTON STATES:
[🔼 BUY UP]  →  Hover: ↑ translateY(-2px) + glow
                Active: ↓ scale(0.98)


ANIMATIONS:
• Header: Shimmer effect (3s infinite)
• Buttons: Ripple on click (expanding circle)
• Cards: Slide & fade on load
• Refresh: Rotate 90deg on hover
• Markets: Slide right 4px on hover


COLOR GRADIENTS:
Primary:   #10b981 → #059669 (Green)
Secondary: #ff444f → #ff6b75 (Red)
Glass:     rgba(15,23,42,0.7) with blur(20px)
